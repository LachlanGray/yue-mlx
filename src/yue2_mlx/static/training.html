Experimental local adaptation.
Uses a small automatically generated dataset for retention, hard-token cross entropy, checkpointed layers and short windows. This is not an exact reproduction of the author’s large-corpus recipe and does not guarantee cleaner audio. With multiple songs, the last song is held out; with one song, its final quarter is held out. Initial preprocessing and supplementary generation can take time. Keep your Mac awake.

Runs one at a time with your audio jobs. Each complete checkpoint appears in Semantic lab (about 300 MiB per pair). Stop requests finish the current update and save changed weights; during preprocessing they finish the current extraction/generation unit. Force-quitting can lose work since the last checkpoint.

Training runs

Stop and save, then use Resume on the stopped run to continue its original schedule, optimizer and dataset. Resume queues a new continuation; form settings apply only to fresh runs. Checkpoints from older versions support warm starts only. Resumable state adds roughly 600 MiB per checkpoint. Losses are noisy window samples, not audio-quality scores.
